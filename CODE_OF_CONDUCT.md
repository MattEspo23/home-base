# Community conduct

Be constructive, specific, and respectful. Critique the work, not the person.
Give others room to learn, state assumptions, and correct mistakes. Do not
harass, threaten, discriminate, expose private information, or pressure someone
into unsafe external actions.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these rules
and may limit participation when needed to keep the project safe. For a concern
that would expose someone publicly, follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md).
