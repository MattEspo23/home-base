# Discover AI opportunities

Use this workflow after orientation or when the user asks what AI could help
with.

## Load

Read the relevant `you/`, `work/`, `projects/`, `skills/`, `tools/`, and
`capabilities/` indexes. Read detailed files only when they contain evidence
for the map.

## Candidate generation

Generate more candidates privately than you show. Reject any candidate that:

- lacks a stated problem or desired outcome;
- depends on invented facts;
- needs unavailable access with no manual alternative;
- creates more maintenance than likely value;
- cannot be tested safely;
- is primarily spectacle.

Show at most:

- two actionable-now choices;
- one clearly labeled saved-later possibility.

## Visible scoring

Score each candidate from 0–5 and explain:

- fit;
- value;
- existing access;
- low effort;
- low maintenance;
- low risk;
- clear proof.

Every candidate must label its evidence source as `user_statement`,
`durable_file`, or `reviewed_work`, then cite a specific statement, file fact,
or reviewed piece of work in at least one full sentence. Make the visible evidence
plain. Do not use “grounded” without showing the evidence.

Use `templates/opportunity-map.md` and write
`opportunities/AI-OPPORTUNITY-MAP.md`. Update `opportunities/INDEX.md`.

## User response

Offer three responses:

1. choose one;
2. refine the map;
3. none fit.

For refine or none fit, ask one question:

> What feels wrong: the problem, the value, the timing, the effort, the access,
> or something I misunderstood?

Record the feedback. Deepen only the relevant branch and revise the map.
Never force a choice or turn the rejected ideas into an automatic backlog.

When the user selects one, follow
`system/workflows/blueprint-a-system.md`.
